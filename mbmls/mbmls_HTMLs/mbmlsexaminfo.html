Exam Information

Your final exam in the course is designed exactly like the quizzes, just more questions to cover all the topics. You will write the 2-hour exam in Moodle. Again, just like the quizzes. The quizzes and exam questions all come from the same very large question bank. So, if you do the quizzes you will know exactly what question style and depth of material will be on the exam. I tend to query understanding rather than fact regurgitation. I do not like applying time-pressure during tests; you should have the opportunity to luxuriate over those questions that seem impossible to answer - and there will be a few of those. They are balanced by others that are so mindlessly obvious that you start to worry.

A page of extra information will be available to you during the exam. (No, you do not need to remember the genetic code.)

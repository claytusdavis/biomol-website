Genomes: an anatomy of information

The entire DNA content of an organism is called its genome. You should understand the organization of the DNA in the genomes of different organisms, its packaging into higher order structure, and the approximate sizes of the genomes and gene numbers in a few of viruses, Prokaryotes, and Humans.
